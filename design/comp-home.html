Shortest tape: Manchester

Manchester is the easiest place to start. Food premises registration is free, cannot be refused, and simply requires 28 days' notice. The company formation process is straightforward (GBP 100 online) with no complex pre-approval chain. There is no activity licence fee, no mandatory pre-opening inspection that blocks trading…

  • AustinModerate complexity and moderate cost; the layered permit system (plan review → pre-opening inspection → operating permit) is transparent and fees are published, but adds up-front cost and sequencing time…
  • ManchesterLowest barriers and lowest fees; free food registration, no mandatory pre-approval inspection, and well-documented compliance pathway make this the most accessible jurisdiction for a first-time café operator.
  • Dubai mainlandHighest regulatory complexity; multiple parallel approval chains (DET trade licence, DM food permit, layout assessment, PIC certification, FoodWatch compliance, UAE corporate tax) with most official fees…
